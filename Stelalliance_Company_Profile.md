## About Stelalliance

Stelalliance (SMC-Private) Limited is a technology group headquartered in Islamabad, Pakistan, building at the intersection of software engineering, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and education innovation. What began as a single product vision has grown into a multi-disciplinary group: custom software and SaaS platforms, AI-driven solutions, and managed cybersecurity services for clients across Pakistan and the Gulf, alongside Stellar Scholar, our space and STREAM education initiative, and Astrobot Academy, the dedicated brand that delivers it in schools.

Our work is guided by a simple idea: the discipline used to defend a system should be the same discipline used to build it, and the curiosity that drives technological progress should be extended to the next generation of scientists and engineers.

### Stellar Scholar — Space & STREAM Education

Stellar Scholar is Stelalliance's space and STREAM education initiative, igniting children's interest in rocket and space sciences through game-based learning, curriculum standards, and a STREAM-aligned educational philosophy. Students explore rocket construction and experiment with components, learning the fundamentals of rocketry through interactive gameplay rather than rote instruction. The initiative runs on a hybrid B2G/B2B subscription model, partnering with government and private school networks, and is brought into classrooms through Astrobot Academy, its dedicated delivery platform.

> **Ministry of Federal Education**
> A collaboration that has engaged more than 20,000 students across schools in the federal capital, Islamabad, with structured space and STREAM learning.

> **International Astronautical Congress**
> Stellar Scholar's work has been presented in published research at the IAC, the world's leading annual gathering of space agencies, industry, and researchers.

---

### Astrobot Academy — The Delivery Engine

Astrobot Academy is Stelalliance's flagship STREAM delivery platform — the operational arm that brings Stellar Scholar's curriculum into real classrooms. Where Stellar Scholar sets the academic framework, Astrobot Academy executes it: deploying structured Robotics, AI, and Space Sciences modules, training and certifying teachers, and managing day-to-day delivery infrastructure across partner campuses. It operates with its own brand identity and contracts directly with school networks to operationalize delivery.

- **Curriculum Delivery** — structured Robotics Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, and Space Sciences modules taught as a compulsory academic subject, not an extracurricular add-on.
- **Teacher Training & Certification** — orientation, capacity-building, and certification programs that prepare educators to deliver the curriculum directly.

> **Notable Partnership — Meezan School System**
> Named as the implementation partner under the Stellar Scholar–Meezan MOU, Astrobot Academy is deploying a compulsory Department of Space Sciences, Robotics & AI from ECE through Grade 8 across a network targeting over 1,000 campuses nationwide, in affiliation with SUPARCO, the Institute of Space Technology, the NASA Space Apps Challenge, and UNESCO.
